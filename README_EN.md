## Clinical facts found (not predicted)

The facts below are reported as found in the sources named; they are analysis results and public clinical records, not predictions and not treatment advice. No treatment decision should be based on them without an oncologist.

- **MTAP / PRMT5 rationale (measured in this project, 4th confirmation):** the PRMT5 effect follows MTAP, not CDKN2A (−1.385 against −1.010, p = 0.0036); the CDKN2A effect disappears once MTAP is fixed (p = 0.76). The vopimetostat + daraxonrasib rationale is strengthened by this analysis; the combination itself is not proven. Sources: white paper §8.46–§8.50; `rapports/prmt5_mtap_dependency.md` and `rapports/prmt5_mtap_9p21_separation.md`.
- **Vopimetostat + daraxonrasib:** no new data on the combination since ESMO 2026; phase 3 without an NCT number (design specified, start first half of 2027). TNG462-C102: ORR 82%, n = 22, no new cut (§8.92). Daraxonrasib alone received European approval on 16 Nov. 2026; first-line daraxonrasib + GnP is more mature (ENA 2026, median PFS 12.8 months) (§8.80).
- **New trial:** NCT07938463 (TAP-ALK: vopimetostat + lorlatinib, ALK+ NSCLC, MTAP-deleted, Dana-Farber, first published 16 Nov. 2026) (§8.92).
- **Supply condition:** Tango's 10-Q of 9 Nov. 2026 states "no assurance" of securing clinical supply of daraxonrasib — the first-half-2027 start is explicitly conditional on supply (§8.92).
- **Drug lot (Chen 2025, 46 PDOs × 63 AUCs):** 2 hits at FDR < 0.05 (paclitaxel rho +0.5085, FDR 0.0191; mitomycin C rho +0.4759, FDR 0.0257); Olaparib/BRCA2 replicated (p = 0.00015); 5/5 CFTR correctors replicated; drug × KRAS and drug × MTAP impossible (no per-PDO table) (§8.84).
